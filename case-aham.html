The Problem

Knowledge exists. Behavior doesn't follow.

Littering, traffic violations, open defecation, noise pollution, civic irresponsibility — Indians already know the rules. Awareness campaigns have never produced lasting behavior change. Civic sense is not a knowledge gap. It is a behavior gap.

The evidence is clear: 40% of households with a working toilet still practice open defecation (NFHS-5). 68.5% of road accident fatalities are in rural areas (MORTH, 2022). ₹1.47 Lakh Cr/yr GDP loss due to traffic accidents alone (NITI Aayog). 13,600+ enforcement actions for littering in Singapore in 2025 — and 60% of Indians pray daily, meaning spiritual identity is widely active and deeply motivating.

"When I recognise my divine nature, my actions must be worthy of divinity. Small actions. Big impact. Together, we change our cities and our destiny."

Infrastructure Alone Fails
RCTs in MP & Odisha
Increased latrine coverage from 9% to 63% — but did not improve usage without behavior change. Infrastructure without identity and habit systems fails.
Awareness Campaigns Fail
Information ≠ Action
Citizens already know the rules. Information alone has never produced lasting behavior change. Awareness without motivation and reinforcement does not sustain.
Punishment Alone Fails
Certainty > Severity
Rare but severe punishments create fear-based compliance that collapses without monitoring. Certainty of detection matters more than severity of punishment.
Behavioral Science Insight
EAST Framework
People change when actions are Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely — connected to identity and visible impact. This is the design target.

The Solution

The 5-JR Framework: Vedic wisdom meets behavioral science

The platform integrates five Vedic principles — Saucha (cleanliness), Ahimsa (non-violence), Maryada (discipline), Rta/Dharma (social order), and Bhumi-Seva (environmental care) — with evidence-based behavioral mechanisms.

Saucha
Cleanliness
Clean surroundings, pure mind, healthy nation.
Ahimsa
Non-Violence
Respect life, ensure safety, stop harm.
Maryada
Discipline
Respect rules, follow queues, value public spaces.
Rta/Dharma
Social Order
Do your duty. Follow what is right.
Bhumi-Seva
Environmental Care
Protect nature, serve the earth.
4-E Model
Sustainable Change
Easy · Attractive · Social · Timely. The quadrant model for sustainable behavior change at scale.

Spiritual to Behavior Mapping

Each spiritual principle maps directly to a psychological mechanism, which maps to a measurable civic behavior. Dharma → Duty Identity → Queue discipline. Karma → Consequence Salience → Anti-littering. Seva → Warm Glow Effect → Volunteering. Ahimsa → Empathy Activation → Safe driving.

Impact at Scale

The change we aim to create

Based on behavioral science interventions and Indian case study data, the projected impact within 12–18 months in pilot cities includes 30–40% reduction in littering, 25–35% increase in public toilet usage, 20–30% decrease in traffic violations, 15–25% increase in civic participation, and 10–20% improvement in waste segregation.

The projected ROI is 12:1 on enforcement and behavior change investment combined, with ₹20,000+ Cr/year potential savings in healthcare and productivity at national scale.
