Market Context

Why India's EV market needs a fundamentally different vehicle

12.55L compact SUV units sold FY24. 65% share of total vehicles occupied by SUVs. 4.3M total passenger vehicles sold FY2024–25. Total electric PVs sold FY2025: 1,07,541 — first time crossing 1,00,000. 95%+ still buying ICE vehicles.

The EV penetration in compact SUV segment is <5% (₹55,000–65,000). No vehicle under ₹25L currently delivers all five white space criteria simultaneously — the gap VAYU is designed to fill.

"VAYU is not just another EV. It is the first EV designed around how India actually lives. Intelligent. Confident. Indian. Electric."

The White Space
5 Criteria Simultaneously
450km+ real range (Indian conditions) · Level 2 ADAS (HDA2) · Flat floor EV architecture · E-GMP 800V architecture · 195mm+ GC (Indian roads). No competitor delivers all 5.
User Research
10 Interviews · 4 Owners · 6 Non-Owners
Age 28–40. ₹12–25 LPA. Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad. The 6-Stage EV Decision Journey mapped across all respondents.

Research Insights

The 6-Stage Decision Journey — longer, more collective, more anxious than petrol

Indian EV buying is collective, not individual. One charger failure shapes household perception for months. 900+ digital touchpoints consulted. 2–4 weeks longer than ICE research. Annual trip to Goa at 40% intent killed here.

Stage 01
Trigger Event
Fuel price spike (₹100+/litre). Colleague buys EV. Mall activation test drive.
Stage 02
Digital Research
900+ digital touchpoints. YouTube, Team-BHP forums, CarDekho comparisons. 2–4 weeks longer than ICE.
Stage 03
Range Maths
Personal route mapping. Commute ×3 buffer. Annual trip to Goa at 40% — intent killed here.
Stage 04
TCO Calculation
₹1.3/km EV vs ₹6.5/km petrol. Break-even 3.5–5.5 years. Higher EMI psychologically punishing.
Stage 05
Apartment Problem
Does my RWA allow charging? 15A socket in my parking? Installation cost? 30% intent killed here.
Stage 06
Family Consensus
Spouse's opinion. Parents' fear. "Go trip kar payenge?" One negative family voice can kill the purchase.

Key Insight: EV buying in India is collective, not individual. The design response: address family anxiety as a primary UX requirement, not an afterthought.

The Design Response

One vehicle. Five regional intelligences. Infinite India.

VAYU is purpose-built around 5 non-negotiable specifications derived directly from research: 450km+ Real-World Range (Indian conditions, not ARAI), Level 2 ADAS (HDA2 highway assist), Purpose-Built EV (E-GMP flat floor, 800V architecture), 195mm+ Ground Clearance, and 20→80% in 35 min fast charging.
