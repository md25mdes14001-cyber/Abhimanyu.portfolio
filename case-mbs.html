A gamified wellbeing system that doesn't optimize engagement

Built a three-domain behavioral system integrating physical, emotional, and nutritional wellbeing — with AI-powered pattern detection and crisis escalation. The wellness industry has confused engagement with outcomes. This corrects the error.

The Research

What we discovered

115 students aged 17–28 were surveyed across India. The data revealed a critical behavioral loop — stress triggers emotional eating, which triggers guilt, which triggers restriction, which feeds back into stress. This cycle continues until it is interrupted by design.

73% reported emotionally eating during stress. 62% felt guilt after eating. 84% identified academic pressure as the key distress trigger. Cultural stigma was identified as the largest barrier to help-seeking.

"Students preferred anonymous, visual, low-text systems. Stigma was the largest barrier to seeking help. The system had to normalize emotional conversations — not pathologize them."

Secondary Research
Rising Subclinical Disorders
Rising cases of subclinical eating disorders and emotional eating among youth in India. Cultural stigma delays help-seeking. Need for non-clinical, accessible tools for early detection and support.
Behavioral Insight
The Distress Loop
Stress → Emotional Eating → Guilt → Restriction → back to Stress. This cycle continues until it is interrupted. The design goal: interrupt the loop at multiple points, not just one.
Design Principle
Anonymous & Visual
Students preferred low-text, visual, anonymous interaction patterns. The WBS Score (Well-Being Sync) was designed as a single, non-judgmental number — not a diagnostic label.

The Solution

Mind Body Sync — a 3-step journey from awareness to well-being

The platform is built around three phases: Detection (understand your current state), Regulation (build healthy habits), and Recovery (access support when needed). Each phase is designed to reduce stigma and increase self-awareness through small, manageable interactions.

The WBS Engine — a single score combining Emotional Stability (40%), Functional Health (30%), and Nutritional Behavior (30%) — gives users a non-judgmental, holistic picture of their wellbeing at a glance.

Step 01
Detection
Food, mood, sleep & activity logs. Emotion check-ins. AI-supported pattern detection. "How are you feeling?"
Step 02
Regulation
Personalized quests. Breathing, grounding, journaling. Small steps. Real progress. Build habits that help.
Step 03
Recovery
Support hub. Peer stories. Professional help pathway. Hero's Badge for seeking help — courage looks different for everyone.

The Heart of the System

WBS Engine: Well-Being Sync Score

A single score that reflects overall wellbeing across three domains — because wellbeing is relational. Any system that addresses only one domain will fail its users precisely when they need it most.

40%
Emotional Stability
Mood, stress, guilt, rigidity, coping patterns. The layer most wellness products either ignore or reduce to a single emoji check-in.
30%
Functional Health
Sleep quality, energy levels, activity patterns, HR variability. Cross-feeds into emotional and nutritional state awareness.
30%
Nutritional Behavior
Meal frequency, timing, and nutritional balance — completing the feedback loop between physical state and emotional wellbeing.
Gamified
Not Superficial
XP Points (consistency), Clarity Gems (reflection), Fuel Points (nourishment), Wiggle Room Points (flexibility). Rewards balance — not perfection.

"Wellbeing is a relational state. Any system that addresses only one domain will fail its users precisely when they need it most."

The change we aim to create

By making self-awareness simple, emotional care approachable, and help-seeking rewarding — the platform aims to shift young adults from distress loops to sustainable well-being patterns.
